# English listing — Mầm 2.1.0

Upload `../mam-edge-store-2.1.0.zip` to update the existing extension. The manifest includes English and Vietnamese metadata. The English listing name is **Mầm • Water & Movement Reminders**.

## Description

Build small, caring habits with Mầm — a friendly water and movement reminder for Microsoft Edge.

- Get reminders to drink water and take movement breaks.
- Set your cup size in milliliters and choose a daily water goal.
- Undo your most recent entry if you tap by mistake.
- Customize reminder days, working hours and lunch breaks.
- Review your hydration and movement progress over the last seven days.
- Grow a cheerful plant through your daily habits. Reach your water goal to make it bloom.
- Pause reminders for 25, 50 or 90 minutes with focus mode.
- Personalize colors, plant pots, system notification sounds and in-page reminder duration.
- Choose desktop notifications or optional reminder cards on the webpage you are viewing.
- Switch between English and Vietnamese using the Language selector at the top of the popup. Your choice is saved automatically.

No account is required. Your preferences and activity records stay on your device and are not sent to external servers.

Microsoft Edge must be running for scheduled reminders. Device sleep may delay notifications. In-page reminders are unavailable on restricted pages and use desktop notifications instead. Desktop banners and sounds depend on your operating system settings.

## What's new in 2.1.0

English support for the entire interface, desktop notifications and in-page reminder cards. Switch languages without changing your history, goals or reminder schedule.
